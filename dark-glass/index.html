Human Digestive System

Science, Class 6 format Block 15, pages 49 to 54

Enzymes are chemicals in our body that break food down into small, simple parts the body can absorb. Each enzyme works on only one kind of food.

1Keywords

  • Digestion: breaking food down into small, simple parts the body can absorb.
  • Enzyme: a chemical that speeds up the breakdown of food.
  • Substrate: the food substance an enzyme acts on.
  • Lock and key: each enzyme fits only one kind of substrate, like a key in its lock.
  • Amylase: enzyme in saliva. Breaks starch into sugar.
