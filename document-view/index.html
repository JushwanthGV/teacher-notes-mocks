    Human Digestive System

    Block 15, textbook pages 49 to 54. Role of enzymes in digestion.

    1.Keywords

    Digestion
    Breaking food down into small, simple parts the body can absorb.
    Enzyme
    A chemical that speeds up the breakdown of food.A protein made by the body that speeds up the breakdown of food. The enzyme itself is not used up.
    Substrate
    The food substance an enzyme acts on.
    Lock and key
    Each enzyme fits only one kind of substrate, like a key in its lock.
    Amylase
    Enzyme in saliva. Breaks starch into sugar.
    EnzymeActs onDigested product
    AmylaseStarchMaltose (a sugar)
    ProteaseProteinsAmino acids
    LipaseFatsFatty acids and glycerol

    2.How food is broken down

    Food is broken down in steps. Each step uses its own enzyme, like a key that fits only one lock.

    3.Practice questions

    Questions 1 to 3 are from the textbook, page 50.

    1. The action of salivary enzymes on food can be best compared to: a. pen and pencilb. lock and keyc. bat and ball
    2. Liana eats grilled chicken for lunch every day. Name one enzyme that helps to digest the food she eats.
    3. How will the absence of the enzyme lipase affect digestion in our body?
    4. Saliva contains an enzyme called ________. It breaks down starch into sugar.
    5. Why can an enzyme that breaks down proteins not break down starch?
    6. Put these in order: stomach, mouth, small intestine, oesophagus.
